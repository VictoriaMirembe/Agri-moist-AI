# User research

**Status: started.** Only one officer conversation so far (Maracha district, West
Nile), not yet in Karamoja. This file is where Evans records notes from talking to an
extension officer or farmer (TASKS.md T06, T33) and the 1-2 changes that come out of
it (T34).

The notes from that conversation, and from a farmer group leader in the same district,
are written up in ["What officers told us" in the README](../README.md#what-officers-told-us):
Dr. Alex Candia, District Production Officer, Maracha district, by phone on 14 November
2026 (he agreed to be named), and a farmer group leader in Maracha on 13 November 2026
(name withheld). The system itself was built before those calls, from the written brief
(RULES.md, DESIGN.md, the hackathon track page) and the team's own judgement.

This is an honest gap, not a hidden one: DESCRIPTION.md and the submission are
expected to name who was actually consulted before the deadline, and to say nothing
that didn't happen (RULES.md section 4).

## Template for the actual interview

- **Who:** name/role, district, date.
- **What they do today** without this tool (paper, memory, WhatsApp groups, etc).
- **What they'd change** about the Visit form, the Approval queue wording, or the
  farmer-facing screens.
- **What we changed** in the build because of it (list the task IDs in TASKS.md).
